Shorts Flow 3.0 · Brand: Tideline

The loop, opened into a line that carries on.

Every Short loops until you swipe. Shorts Flow breaks that loop and carries you to the next one. The mark draws exactly that: a circle that doesn't close, running on downward. Deep night blue, one pearl line, nothing loud.

The mark

One continuous stroke on a 128 grid: a 22-unit loop left open at its lower right, falling into a short arrow. The stroke is 11 units with round ends, the same weight at every size. The tile is a quiet night gradient with a soft sheen from the top left and a hairline edge so it holds on dark toolbars.

Popup

Neutral surfaces carry almost everything. The night card is reserved for Auto-Scroll, with the mark as a faint watermark and the pearl line as its switch. Every other control uses the calmer tide blue, so there is only ever one bright thing on screen.

Store art

No text on the tiles, as the Chrome Web Store recommends, since tiles aren't translated. The marquee pairs the mark with abstract Short frames, the middle one lifted as the Current Short. No YouTube screenshots, logos or red.

Rules

These keep the brand clear of YouTube's marks and keep the extension calm.

YouTube redNo #FF0000 family anywhere off YouTube. Errors use a muted brick, not red.
Play triangle or "S" ribbonThe mark is a broken loop, never a play button or a Shorts-style S.
Brand on youtube.comThe Shorts Control uses YouTube's own look. Pearl and night never appear on the page.
